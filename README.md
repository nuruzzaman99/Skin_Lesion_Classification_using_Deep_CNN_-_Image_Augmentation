Skin cancer accounts for one-third of all cancer diagnoses globally. Over the last few decades, the incidence of skin cancer has increased over the world. By the world health ranking, Bangladesh is 153th ranking. Even though different types of skin cancer may appear to be the same, dermatologists can use computer-assisted diagnostics to help patients and provide appropriate medical treatment. In this work, five types of skin cancer will be diagnosed on clinical images utilizing transfer learning, a cutting-edge approach, and data augmentation to address the problem of class imbalance. We employed CNN-based transfer learning algorithms in this paper. We have used ensemble methods to achieve excellent performance. In several pre-train models, customized layers have been incorporated, and cooldown approaches have been used to identify pretraining behavior and increase accuracy. In this research, a technique is used to artificially expand the available dataset for training a deep learning model. By this method, we got the highest accuracy of 90.5\% on the Xception model, whereas not employing augmentation yielded a maximum of 55.44\% accuracy on the ResNet model.
